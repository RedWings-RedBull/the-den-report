## Standings and tiebreakers

Chicago has a slightly better playoff probability than Detroit (89.9% to 88.2%) but a lower division probability (24.6% to 34%).

This is a team winning on defense with an offense well behind. The simulation still rates Minnesota highest, so the model is leaning on that defense and the 7-2 record already banked.

Detroit is the opposite case. The offense is ranked 6th at 0.107 EPA per play, which backs up the scoring total. The defense is ranked 24th at 0.041 EPA per play allowed, which fits the 199 points against. The Lions' ceiling depends on that unit.

Chicago is balanced and unspectacular: offense ranked 10th at 0.039, defense ranked 8th at -0.023. That supports a good team, but the 7-2 record and +57 differential read a little better than the underlying play.

Green Bay's profile looks stronger than its record. The defense is ranked 7th at -0.039 and the offense is ranked 14th at 0.012, which fits the 9.3 expected wins. Still, a 1.1% division chance and 34.5% playoff chance show how much ground the Packers have to make up, with the 1-3 divisional mark working against them.
