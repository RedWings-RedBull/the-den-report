## Kickoff and setting

The Detroit Lions visit the Miami Dolphins on Sunday, Nov. 8, at 1:00 PM EST at Hard Rock Stadium. The game is outdoors. Both teams have seven days of rest, so neither has a scheduling edge.

Detroit is 5-2 and Miami is 1-6. The Lions are favored by 7 points with a total of 50.5, which is context for how the market sees the gap. Jared Goff starts at quarterback for Detroit and Malik Willis for Miami. Dan Campbell coaches the Lions and Jeff Hafley coaches the Dolphins.

## How the teams compare

Detroit's offense is the stronger unit. It ranks 6th in EPA per play at 0.113 and has a 49.4% success rate. The passing game drives it at 4th in pass EPA, while the run game sits 15th in rush EPA.

Miami's offense is 23rd in EPA per play at -0.045 with a 40.1% success rate. It ranks 21st in both pass EPA and rush EPA.

Neither defense is good. Detroit allows 0.11 EPA per play, which ranks 30th. It is 29th against the pass and 13th against the run. Miami allows 0.131 EPA per play, which ranks last at 32nd. The Dolphins are also 32nd against the pass and 18th against the run.

## Key matchups

- **Detroit pass offense vs. Miami pass defense: Lions.** This is the widest gap on the board. Detroit's 4th-ranked pass offense faces the 32nd-ranked pass defense.
- **Detroit run offense vs. Miami run defense: even.** Detroit is 15th and Miami is 18th.
- **Detroit pass defense vs. Miami pass offense: Dolphins.** Detroit's 29th-ranked pass defense meets a Miami offense that ranks 21st through the air. That is not an imposing passing game, but it is the one matchup where the numbers lean Miami's way.
- **Detroit run defense vs. Miami run offense: Lions.** Detroit is 13th against the run and Miami's rushing attack is 21st.
- **Explosive plays: Lions.** Detroit ranks 3rd and Miami 26th. Against Miami's pass defense, big plays are the most likely way Detroit pulls away.
- **Third downs: Lions.** Detroit ranks 15th and Miami 31st.

Detroit holds the edge in four of the six matchups, one is even, and Miami holds the other. The Lions' weakness is real, though. A 30th-ranked defense by EPA can keep a poor opponent in the game if Detroit's offense stalls.

- 2022, Week 8: Miami won 31-27 in Detroit.
- 2018, Week 7: Detroit won 32-21 in Miami.
- 2014, Week 10: Detroit won 20-16 in Detroit.
- 2010, Week 16: Detroit won 34-27 in Miami.
- 2006, Week 12: Miami won 27-10 in Detroit.

The Lions won both of their trips to Miami in this stretch, in 2018 and 2010. The most recent meeting, in 2022, was a four-point Miami win, and the 2014 game was decided by four as well. Those results say little about this season's teams, which are better measured by the current numbers.

Those numbers point in one direction. Detroit has the better offense, the better record and the best matchup on the board. Miami's offense is poor, but Detroit's pass-defense ranking and the injuries in its secondary give the Dolphins a path to staying close.
