## Kickoff and setting

The Detroit Lions (5-2) visit Hard Rock Stadium on Sunday, Nov. 8, for a 1:00 PM EST kickoff against the Miami Dolphins (1-6). The game is outdoors, and both teams have had seven days of rest. Detroit is a 6.5-point favorite with a total of 49.5, which is context only.

Jared Goff starts at quarterback for Detroit and Malik Willis for Miami. Dan Campbell coaches the Lions and Jeff Hafley the Dolphins.

## How the teams compare

In the Week 9 data, Detroit's offense ranks sixth in EPA per play at 0.113 and has a 49.4% success rate. The passing game drives it: fourth in pass EPA, against 15th in rush EPA.

Miami's offense is well behind. It ranks 23rd in EPA per play at -0.045, with a 40.1% success rate, and is 21st in both pass EPA and rush EPA.

Neither defense is a strength, but Miami's is worse. The Dolphins rank 32nd in EPA per play allowed (0.131), 32nd against the pass and 18th against the run. Detroit is 30th overall at 0.11 allowed, 29th against the pass and 13th against the run.

The Lions should score. Whether their defense can hold up is the open question.

## Key matchups

- **Detroit pass offense vs. Miami pass defense: Lions.** This is the widest gap on the board, with Detroit fourth against a Miami unit ranked 32nd.
- **Detroit run offense vs. Miami run defense: even.** Detroit is 15th and Miami's defense is 18th.
- **Detroit pass defense vs. Miami pass offense: Dolphins.** The Lions rank 29th against the pass and Miami's offense ranks 21st. It is the only matchup that favors Miami, and the edge is modest.
- **Detroit run defense vs. Miami run offense: Lions.** Detroit is 13th against Miami's 21st-ranked rushing attack.
- **Explosive plays: Lions.** Detroit ranks third and Miami 26th.
- **Third downs: Lions.** Detroit ranks 14th and Miami 31st. The edge comes from the size of the gap, not from Detroit's own standing.

Miami's best path is to keep Detroit's offense off the field and hope its passing game, ranked 21st, finds enough against a weak Lions pass defense. The rankings make that a hard path.

## Injury report

Detroit's defense carries most of the damage.

- **Out:** center Cade Mays (ankle), cornerback D.J. Reed (hip), defensive end DJ Wonnum (concussion), linebacker Jack Campbell (neck) and safety Kerby Joseph (knee). Joseph was limited in practice but is still ruled out.
- **Questionable:** wide receiver Amon-Ra St. Brown (rib, limited), running back Jacob Saylors (neck, limited) and running back Sione Vaki (ankle, did not practice).
- **Full practice, no designation:** linebacker Amen Ogbongbemiga, safety Brian Branch and cornerback Rock Ya-Sin.

With D.J. Reed and Kerby Joseph out, a secondary that already ranks 29th against the pass is down two members. St. Brown's status is the one to watch on the offensive side.

Miami's report is lighter. Running backs Jaylen Wright (quadricep, limited) and Kaytron Allen (quadricep, full practice) are both questionable, as is defensive end Malik Herring (neck, limited). Willis, Chop Robinson, Zach Sieler, Kadyn Proctor and the other listed Dolphins practiced fully with no designation. Long snapper Tucker Addington was limited with no status attached.
