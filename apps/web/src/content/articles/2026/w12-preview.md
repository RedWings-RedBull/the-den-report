## Setting and stakes

The Detroit Lions host the Chicago Bears at Ford Field on Thursday, Nov. 26, at 1:00 PM EST. The game is indoors under the dome. Both clubs are 8-2 and both have four days of rest. Jared Goff starts for Detroit and Caleb Williams for Chicago. Dan Campbell and Ben Johnson are the head coaches.

The market has the Bears favored by 2.5 on the road, with a total of 55.5. That is context only. By EPA, Detroit has the stronger offense and Chicago the stronger defense.

## Offense and defense by league rank

Detroit has the clear edge on offense. The Lions rank fourth in EPA per play at 0.136 and have a 51.1% success rate. They are third in pass EPA and seventh in rush EPA. Chicago's offense is mid-table: 11th in EPA per play at 0.026, with a 46% success rate, 14th in pass EPA and 15th in rush EPA.

The defensive side flips. Chicago allows -0.041 EPA per play, seventh in the league. The Bears are seventh against the pass and 13th against the run. Detroit allows 0.05 EPA per play, which ranks 24th. The Lions' run defense is eighth, but their pass defense is 25th.

**Detroit run offense (seventh) vs. Chicago run defense (13th): even.** Detroit has the better rank, but nothing separates the sides.

## Injury report

Detroit's concerns center on a secondary that already ranks 25th against the pass. D.J. Reed (CB, hip), Ennis Rakestraw Jr. (CB, shoulder) and Khalil Dorsey (CB, ankle) are all questionable and were limited in practice. Joe Bachie (LB, knee), Tyler Lacy (DE, shoulder) and Sione Vaki (RB, ankle) are also questionable and limited.

Cade Mays (C, ankle) is out after not practicing. Miles Frazier (G) did not practice, but no status or injury is listed for him. Kerby Joseph (S) practiced fully.

Chicago will be without Kyler Gordon (CB), who is out with a shoulder injury. Zavion Thomas (WR, ankle) is questionable after not practicing. Jeffery Simmons, Kyle Monangai, Luther Burden III and Malik Muhammad all practiced fully and carry no status.
