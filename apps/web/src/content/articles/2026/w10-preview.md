## Kickoff and setting

The Detroit Lions (6-2) host the New England Patriots (5-4) on Sunday, Nov. 15, at 9:30 a.m. EST at FC Bayern Munich Stadium. Detroit is the designated home team, and the game is played under a roof. Both clubs have had seven days of rest. Detroit is a 2.5-point favorite and the total is 49.5, which is context only. Jared Goff and Drake Maye are the quarterbacks. Dan Campbell coaches Detroit and Mike Vrabel coaches New England.

## How the teams compare

Detroit has the better offense. It ranks sixth in EPA per play at 0.102 with a 48.7% success rate. The passing game drives that, ranking fifth in pass EPA. The ground game ranks 13th in rush EPA.

New England is a step behind. It ranks 10th in EPA per play at 0.041 with a 45.5% success rate. It is 10th in pass EPA and 22nd in rush EPA.

Defense is where Detroit is exposed. The Lions allow 0.08 EPA per play, which ranks 28th. They are 27th against the pass and 12th against the run. The Patriots allow 0.043 per play, which ranks 23rd. They are 24th against the pass and 13th against the run.

## Key matchups

- **Detroit pass offense vs. New England pass defense: Lions.** Detroit's fifth-ranked pass offense meets a pass defense ranked 24th. It is the clearest mismatch on the board.
- **Detroit run offense vs. New England run defense: even.** Both sides rank 13th.
- **Detroit pass defense vs. New England pass offense: Patriots.** The Lions' 27th-ranked pass defense faces a 10th-ranked pass offense. This is Detroit's biggest vulnerability, and two of its injured players are defensive backs (see below).
- **Detroit run defense vs. New England run offense: Lions.** Detroit's 12th-ranked run defense faces a 22nd-ranked rushing attack. New England's running back injuries could limit that plan further.
- **Explosive plays: Lions.** Detroit's rank is fifth and New England's is 24th.
- **Third downs: Patriots.** New England's rank is 11th and Detroit's is 21st.
