## How the game was decided

The Detroit Lions beat the New England Patriots 35-10 in Munich as 2.5-point favorites. The margin came from efficiency. Detroit averaged 0.236 EPA per play with a 50.8% success rate. New England managed -0.264 EPA per play and a 33.3% success rate. The Lions' defense graded at 91.071, their offense at 83.929 and special teams at 62.5, for an overall team grade of 85.

New England moved the ball early, and that is where the game could have gone differently. Maye hit Henry for 12 yards, then found Hollins for 26 yards to the Detroit 7. Detroit held the Patriots to a field goal from there. Hutchinson sacked Maye at the DET 15, a play that cost New England 5.3 points of win probability, and Borregales kicked a 33-yarder.
