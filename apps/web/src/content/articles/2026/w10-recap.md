## How the game was won

The Detroit Lions beat the New England Patriots 35-10 at FC Bayern Munich Stadium. They were 2.5-point favorites, and the margin came from a wide efficiency gap. Detroit's offense averaged 0.236 EPA per play with a 50.8% success rate. New England averaged -0.264 EPA per play with a 33.3% success rate.

The team grades agree: 91.071 for the defense, 83.929 for the offense, 62.5 for special teams and 85 overall. The defense was the better unit. It held New England to a field goal and a fourth-quarter touchdown. ([Q4 12:46](/games/2026/week-10/#play-3528))

## Turning points

New England started well. Maye hit Henry for 12 yards and then Hollins for 26 to the Detroit 7, plays worth 0.063 and 0.057 in win probability. The Lions defense then stiffened. Hutchinson sacked Maye for a loss of 2 yards (-0.053 win probability), and New England settled for Borregales' 33-yard field goal.

Detroit's answer was the biggest swing of the game. Jared Goff found Amon-Ra St. Brown for a 56-yard touchdown that added 18.4 percentage points of win probability and 5.268 EPA. ([Q1 6:26](/games/2026/week-10/#play-518))

New England got a lifeline soon after. A 30-yard pass interference penalty on Rakestraw gave the Patriots 2.475 EPA and 0.063 of win probability. Then DeVito was sacked by Derrick Moore for an 11-yard loss, a -2.08 EPA swing. The penalty was New England's best chance to answer, and the sack undid much of it.

Goff then threw a 12-yard touchdown to Isaac TeSlaa early in the second quarter. ([Q2 13:02](/games/2026/week-10/#play-1061)) Jahmyr Gibbs added a 5-yard touchdown run up the left guard. ([Q2 2:12](/games/2026/week-10/#play-1842)) The one real blemish came with 12 seconds left in the half, when Goff's pass intended for Jameson Williams was intercepted by Byard. ([Q2 0:12](/games/2026/week-10/#play-2223)) It cost 0.044 of win probability. Goff and St. Brown connected again in the third quarter on a 28-yard touchdown. ([Q3 8:28](/games/2026/week-10/#play-2700)) New England's 10-yard touchdown pass from DeVito to Larison in the fourth quarter moved win probability by just 0.005. ([Q4 12:46](/games/2026/week-10/#play-3528))
