## Standings and tiebreakers

With 11 weeks played, the NFC North has two leaders. Detroit and Chicago are both 8-2, and the tiebreaker listed for each is division record. That favors the Lions at 2-0 in the division. The Bears are 0-2.

Detroit also leads on the scoreboard. The Lions have scored 288 points and allowed 220, a +68 differential, and they carry a six-game winning streak. Chicago is close behind at +65 (262 for, 197 against) with a five-game streak.

Minnesota is 7-3 and has the most division wins of the four at 3-1. The Vikings' +25 point differential is well below the two leaders, and they enter the week on a one-game losing streak. Green Bay is 6-4 and 1-3 in the division. Its +5 differential (226 for, 221 against) suggests a team playing close to even football, and the Packers also lost last time out.

## Does efficiency back up the records?

**Detroit** is the most lopsided profile. The offense is fourth in EPA per play at 0.136, which supports the 288 points scored. The defense is 24th at 0.05 EPA per play, and because lower is better on that side, a positive figure is a weakness. The record and point differential are real, but they rest on the offense.

**Chicago** looks balanced. The offense is 11th (0.026) and the defense is seventh (-0.041). Nothing here contradicts an 8-2 record, though neither unit stands out the way Detroit's offense does.

**Minnesota** is the biggest question. The offense ranks 29th at -0.091 EPA per play. The defense is sixth at -0.077, and that unit is carrying the team. A 7-3 record on that profile looks fragile, and the +25 differential agrees with that read. The simulations still project 11.1 wins, so the model credits the Vikings for the record they already have.

**Green Bay** has the most even profile. The offense is 14th (0.013) and the defense is eighth (-0.033). The efficiency is better than a 6-4 team with a +5 differential might suggest, and it fits the 9.5 expected wins. The Packers' problem is the 1-3 division mark and the teams ahead of them, not the underlying play.
